\section{Conclusion and Outlook}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{Summary of Findings}

To accurately answer the research questions regarding the power consumption of home to \ac{SME} routers, a dedicated automated test framework was developed.
Using a \ac{DECT}~200 smart plug, a custom Go-based load generator, and a web-based analysis dashboard, five tests were run across four consumer-grade devices (\verb|Fritzbox~7530|, Huawei~EG8245W5-8T, \verb|Asus~RT-AX68U|, and \verb|Alcatel~HH40V|), addressing eight research questions.

Key findings:
\begin{description}
    \item[RQ1:] Port count has a modest effect -- +6--16\% above idle total, with each loaded port adding just 30--175\,mW. The Fritzbox showed non-monotonic behaviour as its \ac{CPU} saturated.
    \item[RQ2:] An idle \ac{GbE} cable costs 265--302\,mW. For the Asus and Huawei, three idle cables cost \emph{more} than running one port at full 1\,Gbps load.
    \item[RQ3:] Power scales sub-linearly -- going from idle to line rate adds only 4.9--7.1\%. Reducing idle power has a greater impact than limiting traffic.
    \item[RQ4:] Bare-minimum power ranges from 1.14\,W (Alcatel) to 6.34\,W (Huawei); with all subsystems idle: 2.30--7.99\,W, or \euro{5}--\euro{18}/year~\cite{bib:eurostat-electricity}.
    \item[RQ5:] Wi-Fi radios add 0.4--2.7\,W idle and cost 7--8$\times$ more per megabit than wired Ethernet under load. Best efficiency: Asus 5\,GHz near (66\,Mbps/W); worst: Huawei (2.6--10\,Mbps/W). Distance effects are device- and band-dependent.
    \item[RQ6:] Fritzbox eco mode saves 474--670\,mW (7--19\%) but throttles Ethernet to $\sim$100\,Mbps/port. Asus and Huawei TX power reduction modes save negligible amounts considering their overall power consumption.
    \item[RQ7:] Even at full combined load, no device exceeded 47\% of its rated maximum. Spec sheet headroom is large.
    \item[RQ8:] Bridge mode is not energy-efficient. The Huawei + Asus combination draws $\sim$16.6\,W -- +85.6\% vs.\ the Huawei standalone at 8.96\,W. The 8.2\,W baseline idle consumption of the Asus router significantly offsets any power savings gained from the Huawei device's reduced workload.
\end{description}

\subsection{Future Work}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Layer~2 switching tests}: Route traffic between two PCs \emph{through} the \ac{DUT} to test the switching fabric rather than the \ac{CPU}.
    \item \textbf{Long-term idle runs}: A 24h+ measurement to check for sleep states or diurnal patterns.
    \item \textbf{More device categories}: Managed switches, mesh systems, and enterprise APs.
    \item \textbf{Additional load types}: ICMP, mixed TCP/UDP, real-world traffic captures.
    \item \textbf{ISP as Gateway tests}: More combinations of routers and bridge modes, to see if the Huawei + Asus result is typical or an outlier.
\end{itemize}
